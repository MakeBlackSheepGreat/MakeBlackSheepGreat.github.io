      <div class="hm-body">
        <a class="hm-title" href="/en/projects"><svg class="hm-ico"><use href="#i-wave"/></svg>Breast ultrasound classification</a>
        <p class="hm-desc">Trained on BUS-BRA and tested externally on BUSI. Two complementary branches weighted 0.573 / 0.427, followed by an area gate of 0.08–0.75 that falls back to the full image when the mask is unreliable; patient-level five-fold splits keep one patient on one side. A stepwise ablation lifts AUC from 0.5996 to 0.9256, with sensitivity 0.8667, specificity 0.8467 and F1 0.7930 on the external set.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="hm-val"><b>0.9256</b><span>AUC · external test</span></div>

    <li class="hm-row">
      <span class="hm-tag"><svg class="hm-ico"><use href="#i-flask"/></svg>In progress</span>
      <div class="hm-body">
        <a class="hm-title" href="/en/projects"><svg class="hm-ico"><use href="#i-scan"/></svg>Chest CT nodule grading</a>
        <p class="hm-desc">The task is nodule classification and malignant risk grading on chest CT. A 2.5D six-channel input (five slices plus one mask) avoids the deployment cost of a 3D network, with patient-level splits, probability calibration and a 19-point operating curve. AUC is 0.8787 on 503 LNDb cases; at threshold 0.30 sensitivity is 0.6176 and specificity 0.9218, at 0.15 they are 0.7794 and 0.8161.</p>
      </div>
