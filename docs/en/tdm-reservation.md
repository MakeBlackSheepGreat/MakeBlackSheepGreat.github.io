## Scope of reservation

The following uses are **prohibited without prior written permission**:

1. **AI training and fine-tuning** — content from this site may not be included in pre-training
   corpora, continued pre-training data, supervised fine-tuning data, or preference-alignment data.
2. **Knowledge distillation** — content from this site, or outputs derived from it, may not be used
   as teacher signals to train, distil, or synthesise data for any model.
3. **Real-time model input** — content from this site may not be fed into retrieval-augmented
   generation (RAG), grounding, or any other real-time context supply for generative systems.
4. **Synthetic corpus generation** — generative models may not rewrite, paraphrase, or expand this
   site's content in order to produce training corpora.
5. **Dataset packaging and resale** — content from this site may not be bundled into any dataset
   product and redistributed or sold.
